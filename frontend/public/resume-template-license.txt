ScoutSearch Resume builder: where the resume layout comes from
==============================================================

The Resume builder (yoursite.com/resume) lays out resumes the way
Jake's Resume does: the name centered at the top, section headings with
a line under them, and dates on the right. ScoutSearch wrote its own
HTML and CSS for this (frontend/src/pages/ResumeBuilder.jsx and the
"Resume builder page" section of frontend/src/index.css). No code from
the template was copied, but its license notices are kept here anyway,
to give credit the way the MIT License asks.

Jake's Resume is itself based on sb2nov/resume, so both notices are below.

The MIT License covers the template, not the resumes people make with
the builder, so nothing from this file needs to appear on a resume.
